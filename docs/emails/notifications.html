Hi Debbie, Bobby and Joe,

The Smelley Pool dashboard can now send notifications to your phone: when someone @mentions you in Smack Talk, when the Commentator answers your question, when your picks finish, and more. You choose what you get, and it starts on the quiet side. Setup takes about 3 minutes. Jamie, Shannon and I are already using it.

Examples of what you might see
WHAT IT CAN DO
💬Smack Talk: when someone @mentions or replies to you (or every message, if you like).
🎙️The Commentator: its answers to your questions, plus (if you want) the weekend preview and weekly recap.
🏈Your picks: the final result of each one; optionally cover flips and late sweats in the last minutes.
🔔Games you follow: open any game and tap Follow this game for its final score, cover changes, or every score.
🏆The family race: your week's total when your last game ends, and when the family lead changes hands.
🌙Your controls: an hourly limit, quiet hours overnight, and hiding details on the lock screen.
📱 On an iPhone
Apple only allows notifications from the app on your Home Screen, so there's one extra step. (Needs iOS 16.4 or newer.)
  1. Open the Smelley Pool in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow ↑), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open Smelley Pool from your Home Screen.
  4. Sign in there: type your email, tap Email me a sign-in link or code, and type the 6-digit code from the email (or use your password).
  5. Tap your name (top right) → 🔔 Notifications → Turn on notifications, then tap Allow.
  6. Tap Send me a test. It should pop up within a few seconds.
🤖 On an Android phone
  1. Open this link to the notification settings in Chrome (or Firefox) and sign in if asked.
  2. Tap Turn on notifications, then Allow.
  3. Tap Send me a test. It should pop up within a few seconds.
  4. Optional: tap the browser's ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or Install) to get a Smelley Pool app icon.
Later, find the settings any time: tap your name (top right) → 🔔 Notifications.
THEN CHOOSE WHAT YOU GET

On the same screen, pick what should buzz you. It saves as you go and applies to all your devices. Out of the box you'll only hear about @mentions, the Commentator's answers to you, your picks' results and your week's total.

Test didn't arrive?
• iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Smelley Pool, make sure Allow Notifications is on, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't on.
• Android: Settings → Apps → Chrome (or Firefox) → Notifications should be on; battery saver can delay them.
• Still stuck? Reply with a screenshot of where, or see Help → Phone notifications in the dashboard.

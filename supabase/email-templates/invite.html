GAME DAY, LIVE
The whole family's week, on one screen.

Points banked, what's still possible and everyone's odds to win the week, updating as the games are played. Lead Watch follows every live game with family points on it: who's covering, by how much, and whose cover is one score from disaster.

THE RACE
Who's winning the week, and who should you root for?

The Pick Lab plays the rest of the weekend out 5,000 times, then shows which games swing the family race, each of us against the whole league, and the family against everyone else. It even flags when someone's gone off-script from their usual way of picking.

RIVALRIES
You vs. every Smelley. All-time.

Head to Head keeps everyone's weekly record against everyone else, this season or all-time. Tap a matchup for the rivalry card: who's ahead, the odds of beating them this week, and the only games that can decide it ("Georgia covers: Jamie +10").

NEWS & INJURIES
Know why the line moved.

Every game card now has the latest ESPN headlines for both teams, the NFL injury report, and how far the betting line has moved since the commissioner's sheet went out. When the quarterback's out and the line jumps four points, you'll know before kickoff.

SCOUTING REPORTS
Tap any name. See how they really pick.

Everyone in the league has a report: how often their picks cover, whether they lean on underdogs or big favorites, how their 10s have done, and what sets them apart. Plus the players just ahead and behind you in the standings, and what they do differently.

AROUND THE LEAGUE
The whole league's storylines, every week.

Who's leading and since when, every lead change, the week's best scores, the biggest climbers and crashes, and where each of us moved. Once the official scores are in, the Commentator posts a weekly recap in the chat.

HISTORY
2024 and 2025, rebuilt pick by pick.

Every card from the last two seasons, graded against the final scores: champions, the family's finishes year by year, and every week's results. Plus the big question: do the "strategies" actually hold up? (Spoiler: going against the crowd paid off in 2025 and backfired in 2024.)
